Bring Leon Home — Quick Facts

As of June 2026

The Case

Leon Levenstein, age 5, is a U.S. citizen separated from his American father, Nicholas Levenstein, and held in Russia since December 2023.

Leon was in Russia with his mother, Anna Lyudniovskova, when she tragically passed away in 2023. Despite Nick's legal parental rights as Leon's father, Russian courts and Guardianship Authority have repeatedly blocked Nick's contact with his son.

Leon has not seen or spoken with his father in nearly three years.

Legal Posture — 6 Active Tracks

  • Constitutional Court (Russia) — Complaint accepted for review Jan 2026 (Petition No. 9488/15-01/2026). Most viable active track.
  • Paternity and Custody lawsuit — pending
  • Visitation — denied by Golovinsky District Court Dec 12 2025; on appeal
  • Criminal complaint against Guardianship Authority officials — pending
  • Hague Convention — original case rejected Dec 2024; Hague-specific appeals ongoing
  • Indonesian Social Ministry inspection documented

Key Dates
